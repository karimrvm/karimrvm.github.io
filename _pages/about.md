I am a doctoral researcher in mathematics at the [University of Hamburg](https://www.math.uni-hamburg.de/en.html), supervised by [Paul Wedrich](https://paul.wedrich.at) and affiliated with the [CRC 1624 “Higher Structures, Moduli Spaces and Integrability”](https://www.sfb1624.uni-hamburg.de/).

Research Interests
======

My research focuses on Khovanov homology and \\(\mathfrak{gl}_N\\) link homology, with a particular emphasis on colored invariants and the link homology of cables. I am interested in both their computational properties and the underlying categorical structures.

My recent work develops methods for computing colored Khovanov homology and colored superpolynomials, together with an accompanying online database. My current work studies cabling in \\(\mathfrak{gl}_N\\) link homology. More broadly, I am interested in the connections between low-dimensional topology, representation theory, and categorification.
